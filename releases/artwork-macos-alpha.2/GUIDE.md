# Preserve how a contribution changes the work

Bring your artistic premise to your coding Agent. Share this guide and
`consumer/examples/stated-intention/proof.mjs` from the bundle. First
[run the example](INSTALL.md). Then give your coding Agent a concrete
brief for a separate scratch Work, such as:

> Offer this original mark unchanged. Let a participating Agent choose
> one way to alter it. Leave room for the Agent to describe its aim, if
> it wishes. Show me the original, what was returned and what the app
> made from it. Keep inspection private while we test it.

Choose the offered material, the expressive decisions open to participants,
what the app can derive and who can inspect each piece. Ask your coding
Agent to model those choices with the SDK owner and the existing evidence
records. Keep the renderer and creative rules in your app.

| What to preserve | What the example does |
| --- | --- |
| Offered material | Stores the exact source SVG in Context, with its media type and digest. It keeps the invitation alongside it. |
| Contribution and optional statement | Records the return in an attributed Act with a Receipt and exact original bytes. One scripted return includes an optional statement. A second contribution passes without one. |
| Later reflection | Records a new Act and Receipt at a later time. An app-declared Relation links it to the earlier contribution and retains the app as declarer. |
| Consequence | The app derives a new SVG from the offered one and the choice. Its transformation Report identifies the source, contribution, rule and output digest. |
| Permitted inspection | A selected account keeps the exact authorized material. The example shows a broader selection and a restricted selection that excludes the later reflection. |

Preserve the attribution and the available statement time. A statement
embedded in a return shares that carrier's disclosure scope. Use a separate
Act and material for a later reflection or when separate disclosure is needed.
Record the source mark as offered material when the app actually offered it.
Label a baseline produced later as a comparison and keep its actual recipe.

The core owns scoped recording, Receipt handling and access checks.
The optional evidence-account module assembles the selected material for
inspection. The app still controls copy permission and presentation.
Treat the SVG as data until your app applies its rendering policy. The
example checks exact bytes and relationships, including which reader can
access a selection. It uses no provider, browser renderer or private chat
material.
