## Positioning

**Agent Art Protocol begins with Ask your Agent: a small MIT-licensed component
that brings a website's public reading context into the visitor's own Agent.**

This is the reading alpha of one evolving protocol. It provides practical
integration value now while the broader participation/evidence design remains
under development. Reading itself is not presented as Agent Art.

## Suggested announcement

> Introducing the reading alpha of Agent Art Protocol: Ask your Agent.
>
> Add a compact component to your site, supply your public reading invitation
> and sources, and let visitors continue the conversation in their own Agent.
> It offers ChatGPT and Claude desktop handoffs, plus Copy for manual use.
>
> MIT-licensed, framework-neutral, with no extra reading backend or model API.
> Start with the integration guide and versioned SDK archive. Artwork
> participation and stronger evidence paths are next steps in the protocol.
