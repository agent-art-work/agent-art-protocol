# Start with your idea and your coding Agent

This is a local SDK alpha for an artist working with a coding Agent. Start by
telling your Agent what you want the work to make possible. You do not need to
begin by learning the SDK's storage implementation. The creative decisions remain
yours; the SDK gives your app a reusable participation and evidence foundation.

Give your coding Agent the prepared snapshot and this brief:

> Read README.md, INSTALL.md, SUPPORT.md, CORE-CONTRACT.md and ACCOUNT-CONTRACT.md.
> Help me build a small participation work from my idea. First agree the offered
> context, the Agent's room to contribute/revise/refuse, the app's treatment and
> what participants may inspect. Use one authoritative SDK owner per Work and
> the supported imports. Start in a fresh local synthetic project with explicit
> fixture identities and scripted responses. Keep original expressions, receiver
> receipts, app decisions and output/display claims separate. Reuse the optional
> account companion for a permitted selected process account. Build and test the
> app with me. Do not send an artistic Agent request, connect accounts, expose a
> service, disclose private material or publish/deploy without my separate scope.

## What you and the coding Agent decide

- **The encounter:** who can participate, what they can offer, and how it can
  continue or branch. A separate curator, alternating turns and a fixed ending
  are optional. Give the Agent consequential choices and preserve explicit refusal.
- **The Work:** retained framing, offered Context, contribution mappings, actual
  app rules, rights and finite retention. Declare what the app actually implements.
- **The result:** text or your own graphic rendering, including what selection or
  transformation changes. Your renderer owns SVG validation and display safety.
- **The account:** exact selected originals/records, declared consequence, missing
  material and audience/copy permissions. Decide disclosure separately from capture.

Your coding Agent writes the app and those artistic choices. The SDK supplies
the owner, exact records/recovery, scoped methods, inspection and optional selected
account assembly. It does not invent your Work, certify intention or run an Agent
for you. There is no ready-to-install creator/curator plugin in this snapshot.

First get a labelled fixture encounter working. Then qualify the app's actual
Agent/client route and actual consent under a bounded scope. The Signatures proof
shows one app's synthetic integration; it does not transfer real-client support
to your app. Keep public publication, deployment and durable hosting as explicit
later choices.
