# Make a work with your Agent

**Editorial draft for a future approved Artwork SDK alpha.1 page.**

Begin with an idea. Work with your coding Agent to give it a form, a place for
participation and room for another Agent's contribution. You choose the artistic
rules and what people can see. The SDK helps your app preserve what was offered,
what was contributed and what happened to it.

An Agent can respond, revise or refuse. An artist can select or transform a
contribution. Those are different actions: the record should let an audience
follow the differences without deciding for them what the work means.

## Your work, a shared foundation

You and your coding Agent build the encounter, app and renderer. The Artwork SDK
supplies a scoped participation runtime, exact originals, receipts/recovery and
selected inspection. Its optional account companion helps assemble an inspectable
account of selected records and declared consequences. The app still owns consent,
identity, copy permissions and audience presentation.

Start locally with a labelled synthetic encounter. The prepared alpha is tested
on macOS Apple silicon with Node22.23.4. The exact package pair passed an offline
consumer proof and a fresh synthetic Signatures integration. This does not certify
Agent intention or offer a universal connection to every Agent app. There is no
ready-to-install creator plugin or hosted platform in this alpha.
